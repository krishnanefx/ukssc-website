Our partners

Support that travels further.

Our work is made stronger by organisations that share knowledge, open doors and help the Singaporean student community stay connected.

Public and institutional supporters

Organisations that connect students with public-service, diplomatic and global-network opportunities.

  • GovTech Singapore ↗

    Singapore’s public-sector technology agency.

  • Singapore High Commission in London ↗

    Singapore’s diplomatic mission to the United Kingdom.

  • Singapore Global Network ↗

    A global network for Singaporeans and friends of Singapore.

Education and community collaborators

Partners that strengthen cultural exchange, professional networks and Singaporean community life.

  • British Council ↗

    The UK’s international organisation for cultural relations and educational opportunities.

  • SYNC.uk

    A community for Singaporean young professionals in the UK.

  • Singapore United Kingdom Association ↗

    A community organisation strengthening ties between Singapore and the UK.
